FAQ

Questions & answers

Everything you need to know before booking your first ride with us.

How do I book a cab with RYDO MOBILITY?
Enter your pickup, drop, travel dates, and select a car class. You can confirm your cab with indicative fares and receive immediate booking assistance.
Are the fares shown final?
The fares presented are upfront indicative estimates based on standard distance and duration. Tolls, state taxes, and parking fees are charged transparently based on actual usage.
Can I book an outstation trip?
Yes, RYDO MOBILITY specializes in one-way and round-trip outstation journeys connecting major cities and rural hubs across India.
How do I get driver details?
Driver information including contact details, vehicle registration number, and live status are dispatched via SMS and WhatsApp at least 2 hours prior to scheduled departure.
What if I need to cancel?
We provide flexible cancellation terms. Free cancellations are available up to a designated time window before pickup. Reach out to our 24/7 helpline for quick assistance.
